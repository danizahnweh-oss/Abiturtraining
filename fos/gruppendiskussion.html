Gruppendiskussion starten

Simuliere eine englische Gruppendiskussion wie in der FOS-Prüfung. Die KI übernimmt die anderen Teilnehmer – du diskutierst auf Englisch mit.

Situation

Task

Task I (Statements): Each participant introduces themselves and makes a brief statement on their personal point of view.

Task II (Discussion): The positions and views are discussed, arguments exchanged. At the end, some kind of agreement has to be reached.

Opinions

Diskussionstipps

  • Beschreibe und erkläre deine Position in der Einleitung
  • Beachte die gesamte Angabe (Situation + Aufgabe)
  • Greife die vorgegebene Situation auf
  • Benutze die vorgegebenen Punkte UND denke dir neue Punkte und Ideen aus
  • Wenn ihr euch unterbrecht, benutzt Redewendungen
  • Achtet darauf, ca. gleich viel zu reden
  • Bezieht euch immer wieder auf Punkte zurück, die vorher genannt wurden

Einleitung (Person 1 eröffnet)

"Hello everybody, we are meeting here today to discuss / to debate / to talk about..." "My name is... and I am of the opinion that we should..." "I am strongly convinced that the best way to solve this problem is to..." "Now would you please introduce yourselves!"

Vorstellung (Person 2-5)

"I feel very honoured to take part in this discussion, my name is... and I..." "Let me say that I appreciate taking part in this discussion, my name is... and I..." "I think I can make a valuable contribution to this discussion..." "Thank you for letting me have my say, my name is..."

Zustimmen / Widersprechen / Nachfragen

"I agree with you on that point, because..." "I see your point, but I think..." "That's an interesting argument, however..." "Don't you think that...?" "I partially agree, but we also need to consider..." "Could you elaborate on that?" "Going back to what ... said earlier..."

Kompromiss / Abschluss

"So, can we all agree that...?" "I think we've found some common ground here..." "To sum up our discussion, we all agree that..." "As a compromise, I would suggest..."
